# NEGATIVE-RESULT DRAFT (not a ready manuscript)

## Abstract
We test whether CAPE's certified pin selection can be computed without materializing candidate and incidence lists, using exact window pricing and bounded column generation, and we prove that the CompleteWindows family is dominance-complete in each regime. On 11 development crops the pre-registered gate was not met (terminal STOPPED_GATE_FAILED).
